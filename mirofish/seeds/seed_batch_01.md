# New Investors Circle: Member Profiles (batch 1)

The New Investors Circle is a free six-week workshop at a public library for adults who are just starting to invest for the long term. These members agreed to share a short profile so the group could get to know one another.

## Ben Morales

Ben Morales is a 29-year-old dentist (single, lives alone). Their income is stable and high; debt payments take about 3% of it, and they have about 24 months of expenses saved. No one depends on them financially. They want to invest about $97,000 for retirement, needing it in about 30 years.

They describe themselves as someone who wants maximum growth and insists losses don't scare them. When markets turn rough they see a bargain and add more. They have never been through a big investment loss. Their knowledge of investing is low.
