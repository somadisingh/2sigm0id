# New Investors Circle: Member Profiles (batch 2)

The New Investors Circle is a free six-week workshop at a public library for adults who are just starting to invest for the long term. These members agreed to share a short profile so the group could get to know one another.

## Andre Reed

Andre Reed is a 40-year-old hairstylist (married, no children). Their income is partly commission-based; debt payments take about 14% of it, and they have about 2 months of expenses saved. No one depends on them financially. They want to invest about $15,000 for a bigger home, needing it in about 10 years.

They describe themselves as someone who wants maximum growth and insists losses don't scare them. When markets turn rough they see a bargain and add more. In a past downturn they held on. Their knowledge of investing is low.
