# New Investors Circle: Member Profiles (batch 3)

The New Investors Circle is a free six-week workshop at a public library for adults who are just starting to invest for the long term. These members agreed to share a short profile so the group could get to know one another.

## Hana Davis

Hana Davis is a 42-year-old marketing coordinator (single, shares an apartment). Their income is stable; debt payments take about 19% of it, and they have about 1 months of expenses saved. No one depends on them financially. They want to invest about $13,000 for financial independence, needing it in about 15 years.

They describe themselves as someone who wants maximum growth and insists losses don't scare them. Watching their own balance shrink makes them anxious, and they tend to cut their losses. They have never been through a big investment loss. Their knowledge of investing is low.
