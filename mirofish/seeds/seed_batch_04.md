# New Investors Circle: Member Profiles (batch 4)

The New Investors Circle is a free six-week workshop at a public library for adults who are just starting to invest for the long term. These members agreed to share a short profile so the group could get to know one another.

## Rafael Jackson

Rafael Jackson is a 41-year-old hospital administrator (married, no children). Their income is very stable; debt payments take about 9% of it, and they have about 12 months of expenses saved. No one depends on them financially. They want to invest about $93,000 for growing long-term wealth, needing it in about 25 years.

They describe themselves as someone who is fairly cautious and gets uneasy with big swings. When markets turn rough they stay calm and wait it out. They have never been through a big investment loss. Their knowledge of investing is medium.
