# New Investors Circle: Member Profiles (batch 5)

The New Investors Circle is a free six-week workshop at a public library for adults who are just starting to invest for the long term. These members agreed to share a short profile so the group could get to know one another.

## Aisha Lee

Aisha Lee is a 45-year-old UX designer (married, two teenage children). Their income is stable; debt payments take about 16% of it, and they have about 6 months of expenses saved. Two people depend on them financially. They want to invest about $20,000 for retirement, needing it in about 20 years.

They describe themselves as someone who is comfortable taking real risk for higher growth. When markets turn rough they stay calm and wait it out. In a past downturn they held on. Their knowledge of investing is medium.
